Bhargav N Bhatt

Senior Protocol Engineer

Morpho

I design, develop and formally verify DeFi protocols at Morpho. Check out some of our active projects: Midnight, a fixed-term lending protocol, and Vault-V2, Morpho's modular and non-custodial vault.

Previously, as a Blockchain Researcher at the Web3 Foundation, I worked on interoperability, censorship resistance and DAO governance in Polkadot.

My academic background is in formal methods, with research positions at ETH Zurich, the Software Institute, USI Lugano and Microsoft Research India. I obtained my integrated BS-MS in Mathematics and Computing from IIT Kanpur.
